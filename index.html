E-Commerce & Unit Economics +$25K Profit Recapture

D2C E-Commerce Sales & Unit Economics Dashboard

The Problem

Multi-channel ad spend was scaling without verified unit economics, risking margin erosion as discounts masked declining repeat-customer profitability across 3,500 transactions.

The Scope

Audited $296.9K in Net Sales, resolving 30 duplicate records and 124 nulls. Engineered automated Excel/Google Sheets models tracking CAC vs. ROAS identities, product contribution margins, and cohort repurchase behavior.

The Outcome

Uncovered repeat margin compression (36.27% vs. 36.95%) and delivered an executive financial control model projecting $25K+ in annual profit recapture.

Telecom & Subscription Analytics $548K ARR Protected

Telecom Customer Churn & Revenue Risk Analysis

The Problem

Annual subscription churn quietly threatened recurring revenue, with management lacking quantitative visibility into behavioral indicators before customer cancellation.

The Scope

Audited 50,200 subscriber records in Python (Pandas, Seaborn). Quantified churn correlation across payment failure surges (+95%), support escalations (+74%), and customer inactivity duration.

The Outcome

Pinpointed the critical 25.9 vs. 12.8 inactivity day threshold for early intervention, modeling a targeted retention strategy to protect $548K in recurring ARR from a $3.65M total churn bleed.
